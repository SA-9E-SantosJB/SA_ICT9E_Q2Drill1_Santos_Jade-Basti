A Beginner's Guide to Muay Thai


Muay Thai is a combat sport that originated in Thailand. It is often referred to as "The Art of Eight Limbs" because it uses punches, kicks, elbows, and knee strikes.

Basic Rules
  • Boxers must wear protective gear.
  • Matches are typically three rounds.
  • Strikes to the head are allowed.
Techniques
  • Punches and kicks
  • Elbow strikes & knee strikes
  • Clinching & leg sweeps
Equipment
  • Boxing gloves
  • Hand wraps
  • Mouthguard & shin guards
